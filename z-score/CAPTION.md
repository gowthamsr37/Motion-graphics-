You scored 80 in Maths and 70 in Science. Which is the better result? 👀 Maths looks better… but it's actually Science! The trick is called a Z-SCORE.

📏 A z-score tells you how far a value is from the mean, measured in standard deviations (not in marks).

🧮 The formula: z = (value − mean) ÷ SD

✏️ Worked out:
• Maths: mean 75, SD 10 → (80 − 75) ÷ 10 = 0.5 (just half an SD above)
• Science: mean 60, SD 5 → (70 − 60) ÷ 5 = 2 (two whole SDs above!)
🏆 Compared fairly, Science is the stronger result.

🧭 How to read it:
➕ Positive = above the mean, ➖ negative = below
0️⃣ Zero = exactly average
📈 The bigger the size, the more unusual the value

🛠️ Where it's used:
• For normal data, ~95% of z-scores fall between −2 and 2
• Beyond ±3 is a common outlier flag 🚨
• In machine learning, features like age and salary are turned into z-scores so they share one scale
