You guess every answer on a 10-question true/false test ✍️ What's the chance you get 7 right? 🤔 The BINOMIAL DISTRIBUTION tells you.

📖 The definition, in simple words:
1️⃣ It counts the SUCCESSES
2️⃣ in a FIXED NUMBER of tries
3️⃣ each try has TWO OUTCOMES (right or wrong)
4️⃣ and the SAME CHANCE of success every time

🪙 Start small: flip a coin 3 times and count the heads. 8 possible results → 0 heads: 1 way, 1 head: 3 ways, 2 heads: 3 ways, 3 heads: 1 way. That's a binomial shape!

📝 Back to the test (10 tries, each a 50-50 guess):
• 5 right is the most likely, about 1 time in 4
• 7 right: about 12%
• All 10 right: less than 1 in 1,000

🔁 Change the chance: with 5 options per question, a guess is right only 1 time in 5. The bars shift left, and 2 right becomes the most likely.

🎯 The average is simple: tries × chance
• 10 × 1/2 = 5
• 10 × 1/5 = 2
