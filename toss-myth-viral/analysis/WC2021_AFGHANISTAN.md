# 2021 T20 World Cup — the five Afghanistan matches added by hand

Cricsheet withholds matches involving Afghanistan's men's team, so the open data has 40 of the 45 results.
The five missing games were added from official match reports:

| Date | Venue | Match | Toss | Result | Toss winner won? | Chasing side won? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25 Oct | Sharjah | AFG v SCO | AFG, bat | AFG won by 130 runs | yes | no |
| 29 Oct | Dubai | AFG v PAK | AFG, bat | PAK won by 5 wkts | no | yes |
| 31 Oct | Abu Dhabi | AFG v NAM | AFG, bat | AFG won by 62 runs | yes | no |
| 3 Nov | Abu Dhabi | IND v AFG | AFG, field | IND won by 66 runs | no | no |
| 7 Nov | Abu Dhabi | NZ v AFG | AFG, bat | NZ won by 8 wkts | no | yes |

- **Whole tournament:** toss winners won 28 of 40 in the data, plus 2 of these 5, giving **30 of 45**.
- **Dubai:** toss winners won 11 of 12 plus 0 of 1 (11 of 13). The chasing side won 11 of 12 plus 1 of 1, giving **12 of 13**.
