Win the toss = win the match? 🪙 Captains believe it. The data doesn't.

We checked 8,408 men's matches. Toss winners won… 50.9%. Basically a coin flip.

ODIs: exactly 50.0%. T20Is: 50.5%. IPL: 51.6%.

So where did the myth come from? The 2021 T20 World Cup. In Dubai, chasing teams won 12 of 13, and toss winners took 30 of 45 games, including the final. The next three World Cups: 39%, 56%, 43%. Back to a coin flip.

The one real edge? Tests. Win the toss and bat first, and you win 57% of decided Tests. Nobody wants to bat last on a day-5 pitch. And in the IPL, it's the call that counts: bowl first 55%, bat first 45%.
