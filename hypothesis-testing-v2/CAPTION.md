# Instagram caption

A chips brand says every packet has 50 g 🥔 You weigh 25 packets and the average is only 48 g. Are they cheating, or is it just normal variation? HYPOTHESIS TESTING decides 👇

🎤 INTERVIEW ANSWER (textbook definition): "Hypothesis testing is a statistical method that uses sample data to decide between two competing claims about a population: the null hypothesis and the alternative hypothesis."
💬 In plain words: is the pattern in the data real, or just chance?

🔵 H0, the null: nothing special is going on (the packets really average 50 g)
🟠 H1, the alternative: something is going on (they average less)
We always start by assuming H0 is true.

👥 Every test has this pair:
• A coin: fair vs biased
• A website button: no change vs more clicks
• A new teaching method: no difference vs higher scores

⚖️ It's like a courtroom: innocent until proven guilty. Strong evidence → reject H0. Not enough evidence → "not guilty", which is NOT the same as innocent.

🪜 The 4 steps:
1. State H0 and H1
2. Pick a significance level (usually 5%)
3. Calculate the p-value
4. Decide

1️⃣ Chips: if the packets really averaged 50 g, an average as low as 48 g would happen only about 1% of the time → 1% < 5% → reject H0. The packets really are underfilled.
↪️ But if the average had been 49.5 g, that happens about 27% of the time by chance → fail to reject H0. That doesn't prove the packets are full, there's just not enough evidence.
2️⃣ Coin: 9 heads in 10 flips. A fair coin does that (or better) only about 1% of the time → reject H0. The coin is probably biased.
