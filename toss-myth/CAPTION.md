Does winning the toss actually win matches? 🪙 MYTH BUSTERS #01

We checked 8,408 men's matches (Tests, ODIs, T20Is and the IPL) to find out.

ODIs: toss winners won 1,244 of 2,487 decided games, exactly 50.0%. T20Is: 50.5%. IPL: 51.6%. Every one of them is inside the range a fair coin gives you.

So why does everyone believe it? The 2021 T20 World Cup. In Dubai, the side batting second won 12 of 13 games, and toss winners took 30 of 45 matches. The next three World Cups? 39%, 56%, 43%.

"Toss grounds" are a trap too. The 8 grounds where the toss looked strongest early on (64%) dropped to 42% in their next matches. Small samples tell big lies.

The exception: Tests. Win the toss and bat first, and you win 57% of decided Tests. In the IPL, it's the decision that matters: bowling first wins 55%, batting first 45%.

Across every format: 50.9%. Myth busted… mostly.
