KL Rahul's Test career has never been a straight line. 📈📉📈

Today at Wellington: 136 off 290 balls. His 13th Test hundred, in his first ever Test in New Zealand. That made him the first Indian opener with Test hundreds in all four SENA countries.

THE RISE (2014–17): 3 & 1 on debut, then 110 at Sydney a week later. Seven fifties in a row in 2017, equalling a world record. Out for 199 at Chennai.

THE CRASH (2018–19): 22 innings at 23.5. Without the 149 at the Oval, 17.3. Dropped in 2019, and out of the Test side for almost two years.

THE COMEBACK (2021–24): hundreds at Lord's and Centurion, then 101 at No.6, again at Centurion. But he averages 26.7 at No.3 or lower against 39.3 as an opener.

THE RENAISSANCE (2025–): since Leeds, 22 innings, five hundreds, an average of 55.6.

THE PARADOX: he averages 42.0 at home and 35.5 away, yet 10 of his 13 hundreds came away from home. Four of them in England.
