Heights in a class. Marks in an exam. Even coffee poured by a machine ☕ Measure enough of them and the SAME shape appears: a bell 🔔

📈 It's called the NORMAL DISTRIBUTION (a.k.a. the bell curve), the most famous shape in statistics.

The shape:
⚖️ Symmetric, with one peak in the middle
🎯 Mean = median = mode, right at the centre
〰️ Tails thin out on both sides

🎛️ Just 2 numbers draw it:
• The MEAN sets where it sits
• The STANDARD DEVIATION sets how wide it spreads (small SD = tall & narrow, big SD = short & wide)

✨ The 68-95-99.7 rule:
• ~68% of values fall within 1 SD of the mean
• ~95% within 2 SD
• ~99.7% within 3 SD

📏 Example: heights average 165 cm with SD 7 cm → 68% are 158–172 cm, 95% are 151–179 cm.

🚨 Beyond 3 SD is rare (about 3 in 1,000). That's why it's used to flag outliers and for quality control in factories.
