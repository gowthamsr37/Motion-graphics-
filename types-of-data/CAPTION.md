Your height, your city, your exam rank… all data, but NOT the same type 👀

📊 NUMERICAL (quantitative)
• Discrete: counted → 5 students (never 3.5!)
• Continuous: measured → 165.8 cm, 31.6 °C

🏷️ CATEGORICAL (qualitative)
• Nominal: just names → blood group, city
• Ordinal: names in order → low < medium < high
