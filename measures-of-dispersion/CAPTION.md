Two batters both average 40… so they're equally good, right? Not even close 👀

🏏 A: 33, 39, 41, 47 · B: 5, 35, 45, 75 (same average, totally different spread)

📏 RANGE: highest − lowest → A 14, B 70 (quick, but one freak score fools it)
🟩 VARIANCE: average squared distance from the mean → A 25, B 625
📐 STANDARD DEVIATION: √variance, the typical distance → A 5, B 25

📊 For bell-shaped data, about 68% of values sit within 1 SD of the average.
