A river is 3 feet deep on average… would you walk across it? 🌊 Averages alone can mislead you 👀

Every dataset answers two questions:
🎯 CENTRE (central tendency): where's the middle? Mean, median, mode
↔️ SPREAD (dispersion): how scattered is it? Range, variance, standard deviation, IQR

Which centre?
🏷️ Categories (phone brands, cities) → MODE
⚖️ Balanced numbers (heights, test scores) → MEAN
💰 Outliers / skewed (salaries, house prices) → MEDIAN
(One ₹2L earner drags a city's average to ₹50K… the typical salary is ₹30K.)

When do you need the spread? Whenever consistency or risk matters.
🛵 Two delivery apps both average 30 min. SD 3 min vs SD 15 min: pick the consistent one!

💡 Pro tip: pair them up
• Mean ± standard deviation: both react to outliers
• Median + IQR: both resist outliers
