# Instagram caption

How can a poll predict how CRORES of people will vote by asking just a few thousand? 👀 It doesn't ask everyone. It asks a SAMPLE.

👥 POPULATION = everyone you want to know about
🥄 SAMPLE = the smaller group you actually measure
(Like tasting one spoonful to judge a whole pot of soup 🍲)

🤔 Why not measure everyone? It's often too slow, too costly, or impossible. To test how long every bulb lasts, you'd burn out every bulb 💡

📌 The population's true mean is a PARAMETER (usually hidden). The sample mean is a STATISTIC, our best guess.

🎯 Take another sample and you get a slightly different mean: every sample wobbles around the truth. Bigger samples wobble less → better guesses.

🎲 A sample must be RANDOM. Survey only people at a gym about exercise and your answer is way off. That's BIAS.

➗ One more detail: for a sample's standard deviation, divide by n − 1, not n, so the sample doesn't underestimate the spread.
