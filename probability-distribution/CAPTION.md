Roll a dice. What will you get? 🎲 You can't know for sure… but you CAN know the chance of every outcome 👀 That's a PROBABILITY DISTRIBUTION.

📋 A probability distribution lists every possible outcome and how likely each one is. All the chances always add up to 1 (100%).

There are 2 kinds:
🔢 DISCRETE: for things you count (dice, customers, goals)
📏 CONTINUOUS: for things you measure (height, time, weight)

🎲 Discrete, bars:
• One fair dice: 6 outcomes, 1/6 each, and the bar heights are the probabilities
• Two dice: 36 combinations, and 6 of them make 7 → 7 is the most likely total (6/36), while 2 and 12 are the rarest (1/36)

📈 Continuous, curves:
• The chance of one exact value (like exactly 165.000… cm) is zero
• Instead, the chance is the AREA under the curve: heights between 158 and 172 cm ≈ 68%
• The total area is always 1

🧭 Why it matters: distributions turn uncertainty into numbers you can plan with: customers per hour, battery life, the chance of rain.
