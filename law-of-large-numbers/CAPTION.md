Every night someone walks out of a Goa casino with lakhs 💰 And yet the casino itself NEVER loses. Not this month, not this year. Rigged? No. It's just maths 👇

🎰 THE TINY EDGE
Roulette has 37 pockets: 18 red, 18 black, 1 green zero.
Bet on red → you win 18/37 = 48.6%. The house wins 51.4%.
That one green zero = a 2.7% house edge → for every ₹100 bet, the casino keeps ₹2.70 on average.

🪙 THE COIN TEST
10 flips → 70% heads (way off)
100 flips → 54%
1,000 flips → 50.8%
10,000 flips → 50.1%
The more you flip, the closer you get to the true 50%.

📖 THE LAW OF LARGE NUMBERS
As the number of trials grows, the average result gets closer and closer to the expected value.
Interview answer: the sample mean converges to the population mean as the sample size grows.

🏦 BACK TO THE CASINO
10 lakh bets × ₹100 = ₹10 crore bet → 2.7% edge = ₹27 lakh expected.
With that many bets, the real profit lands between about ₹25 and ₹29 lakh almost every time.
Short term: luck. Long term: maths.

🌍 IN REAL LIFE
🚗 Insurance: nobody knows WHICH driver will crash, but across 10 lakh drivers the company knows almost exactly HOW MANY will. That sets your premium.
🏏 Cricket: an average after 3 innings means nothing. After 100 innings, it's the real player.
⭐ Ratings: 5.0 from 3 reviews? Don't trust it. 4.6 from 5,000 reviews is the real picture.

⚠️ THE GAMBLER'S FALLACY
After 5 reds in a row, black is NOT "due". The wheel has no memory; every spin is still 48.6%. The average evens out because millions of new spins drown out the old ones, not because the wheel corrects itself.
