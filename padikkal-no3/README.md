## Data and method

- **Tests:** [Cricsheet](https://cricsheet.org) ball-by-ball JSON (ODC-BY), downloaded on 16 Nov 2026. The scripts are `analysis/extract.py` and `analysis/no3.py`.
  - India's No.3 in each innings is the third batter to appear, taken from the ball-by-ball order.
  - "Since Pujara" starts after his last Test, the WTC final of 7 June 2023.
- **Added by hand:**
  - Padikkal's 80 off 175 at Wellington (Nov 2026).
  - Sai Sudharsan's 81 at No.3 in the June 2026 Afghanistan Test, which Cricsheet withholds. His runs and average include it; his balls per dismissal (71) and strike rate do not, because there is no ball data for it.
- **Totals:**
  - Padikkal in all Tests: 5 Tests, 7 innings, 498 runs, 7 dismissals, average 71.1. His debut innings was at No.4.
  - Padikkal at No.3: 6 innings, 433 runs, 767 balls.
  - The other No.3s since Pujara: 1,620 runs in 51 innings at 33.8 (including Sai's 81), and 61 balls per dismissal from ball-by-ball data.
  - Pujara at No.3: 155 innings, 6,529 runs, 101 balls per dismissal, strike rate 43.9.
- **First-class:** ESPNcricinfo figures as shown in the Wikipedia infoboxes. Padikkal: source dated 19 Aug 2026. Sai Sudharsan: source dated 9 Jul 2026. Stats sites disagree on these numbers, which is why the narration says "both average in the forties, both with ten hundreds" rather than quoting exact figures.
- **Wellington context:** reports say the 80 is the second-highest score for India at No.3 at the Basin Reserve, behind Ajit Wadekar's 143 in 1968.
