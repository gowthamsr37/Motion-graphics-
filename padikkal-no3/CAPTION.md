India have found their No.3. 🏏

Since Cheteshwar Pujara's last Test in June 2023, India have tried seven different No.3s. Only one has made it look easy: Devdutt Padikkal.

His last three first-innings scores: 167 at Galle, 117 in Colombo and 80 in Wellington.

AVERAGE AT No.3 SINCE PUJARA: Gill 37.4 · Sai Sudharsan 31.9 · Karun Nair 27.8 · PADIKKAL 72.2. The benchmark, Pujara's career average at No.3, is 44.4.

THE ROAD: 65 on debut at Dharamsala (batting at No.4) → 0 & 25 at Perth → 21 months out of the side → 167 at Galle, his first Test hundred → 117 in Colombo → 80 in Wellington. For India at No.3 at the Basin Reserve, only Ajit Wadekar has made more (143 in 1968).

TIME AT THE CREASE: Pujara faced 101 balls per dismissal at No.3. India's other No.3s since him have managed 61. Padikkal: 128, and at a faster strike rate than Pujara (56.5 against 43.9).

PADIKKAL v SAI SUDHARSAN: almost identical in first-class cricket (averages of 44.6 and 42.9, ten hundreds each). In Tests, 71.1 against 31.9, and two hundreds against none. Sai is only 25, and his time may come.

Seven Test innings is still a small sample. But every number points the same way.
