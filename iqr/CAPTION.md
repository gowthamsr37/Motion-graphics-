Your commute takes 18 to 60 minutes… so it varies by 42 minutes? Not really 👀 Meet the IQR, the number we keep using in this series, finally explained!

📦 IQR = InterQuartile Range: the range of the MIDDLE HALF of your data. Drop the lowest 25% and the highest 25%, then measure what's left.

🔢 Quartiles cut sorted data into 4 equal parts
• Q1: 25% of values are below it
• Q2: the median (50%)
• Q3: 75% of values are below it
• IQR = Q3 − Q1

🧮 How to calculate it (10 commute times)
1️⃣ Sort: 18 20 22 22 25 | 27 30 30 35 60
2️⃣ Median: (25 + 27) ÷ 2 = 26
3️⃣ Split at the median: Q1 = middle of the lower half = 22, Q3 = middle of the upper half = 30
4️⃣ Subtract: 30 − 22 = 8 minutes

✅ A worse jam (60 → 90) stretches the range from 42 to 72… the IQR stays 8.

Where it's useful:
💰 Skewed data (salaries, house prices): huge values inflate the SD, not the IQR
📊 Box plots: the box IS the IQR
🚨 Catching outliers: anything beyond 1.5 × IQR from the box
