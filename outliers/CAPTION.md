# Instagram caption

9 friends in a café earn ₹40,000 a month on average. A billionaire walks in… and the average jumps to ₹1 crore 🤯 Nobody in the room earns that 👀

That billionaire is an OUTLIER: a value that sits far away from the rest of the data.

Two kinds:
⚠️ MISTAKES: typos, glitches, wrong units (age 25 typed as 250)
💎 REAL BUT RARE: a billionaire, a festival sales spike, a fraudulent payment

🔍 How to spot one: the IQR fence rule
• Find Q1 and Q3: IQR = Q3 − Q1
• Fences = Q1 − 1.5×IQR and Q3 + 1.5×IQR
• Anything outside the fences is an outlier (that's what the dots on a box plot mean!)

📉 How they wreck your analysis
• Drag the average (mean ₹40K → ₹1 crore, while the median barely moves: ₹41K)
• Inflate the spread (range ₹18K → almost ₹10 crore)
• Tilt trend lines (one odd point flattened +6.6 marks/hour to +0.6)
• Squash your charts (one giant bar flattens the rest)
