Does winning the toss actually win you the match? 🪙

You'd think so, the way captains celebrate it. But toss winners won:
• 54.3% of decided Tests
• 49.6% of ODIs
• 50.3% of T20Is
• 51.6% in the IPL

So you're the captain of an IPL side. It's a night game and there might be dew later. You've won the toss. Bat first or bowl first? 🤔

In the IPL, toss winners who chose to bowl first won 54.7%. Those who batted first won 45.3%. That's an association, though, not proof. Captains choose based on conditions, and those same conditions shape the result.

Then it gets stranger. In 2024, IPL toss winners won only about 44% of matches. In 2025 it was about 61%. Same coin, completely different results.

Research ("Fairly Random", Sood & Willis) found almost no average toss advantage in daytime international limited-overs cricket. In day-night games, though, the estimated advantage was +3.3 percentage points, and in Tests it was +4.9. Pitches wear, the weather turns and conditions change.

The toss starts the match. It doesn't decide the winner. Would you have batted or bowled? Tell us below 👇

Sources: HTFT Predictions toss analysis, CricketStudio toss-effect research, Sood & Willis "Fairly Random", Cricsheet. Win rates count decided matches only (draws, ties and no-results excluded). International figures cover men's matches between ICC Full Members. The effects in percentage points are estimated changes in win probability, which is a different statistic from the win rates.
