**Title (YouTube Shorts):**
What If Everyone on Earth Jumped at the Same Time? 🌍

**Description:**
8.3 billion people. One perfectly synchronized jump. Would we shake the planet, trigger an earthquake, or knock Earth out of its orbit?

The physics:
- Earth (5.97 × 10²⁴ kg) is about 12 trillion times heavier than all of us combined (≈ 5 × 10¹¹ kg).
- Even if everyone jumped from one spot, Earth would shift about 0.00000000000003 m, which is thousands of times smaller than an atom. Then it moves back when we land.
- Spread around the globe, our pushes partly cancel out.
- Billions of small, scattered taps are nothing like one earthquake.

What should we test next? 👇
