A fair dice 🎲 A lucky draw 🎟️ A random number on your phone 📱 What do they have in common? 👀 Every outcome is equally likely. That's the UNIFORM DISTRIBUTION.

📖 The definition, in simple words:
1️⃣ Every possible outcome
2️⃣ gets the same chance
3️⃣ so the shape is FLAT, like a rectangle

🎲 Discrete: a fair dice has 6 outcomes, each with a chance of 1/6 → six equal bars.

🚌 Continuous: a bus comes every 10 minutes and you reach the stop at a random time. Your wait can be anything from 0 to 10 minutes, all equally likely → a flat rectangle.

📐 For continuous data, chance = AREA:
• The rectangle is 10 wide and 1/10 tall → total area = 1
• Waiting less than 3 minutes = 3/10 = 30%

🎯 The average is right in the middle: 3.5 for the dice, 5 minutes for the bus.

💻 Fun fact: computers make random numbers with a uniform distribution.
