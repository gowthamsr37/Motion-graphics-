Does winning the toss actually win matches? Let's run the hypothesis test. 📊 DATA LAB #01

H₀: the toss is a coin flip (toss winners win 50%).

We tested 7,965 decided men's matches (Tests, ODIs, T20Is and the IPL).

ODIs: 1,244 of 2,487, exactly 50.0%, p = 0.98. T20Is 50.5%, IPL 51.6%. Both sit inside the range a fair coin gives you. Only Tests break out: 54.2%, p = 0.02.

Why does the myth feel true?
1️⃣ Cherry-picking: 2021 T20 World Cup toss winners won 30 of 45. The next three World Cups: 39%, 56%, 43%.
2️⃣ Regression to the mean: the 8 strongest "toss grounds" fell from 64% to 42% in their next matches. Correlation across 52 grounds: −0.09.

Segment the data and the real effect shows up. It's the decision, not the coin: bat first in Tests → 57%. Bowl first in the IPL → 55%.

All formats: 50.9% (95% CI 49.8–52.0). We fail to reject the null. Verdict: mostly noise.
