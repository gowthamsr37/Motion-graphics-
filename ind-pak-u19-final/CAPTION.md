3 runs. 1 ball. 1 wicket. 🏏

India U19 were 252/8 chasing 338 in the Asia Cup final. Then Mohit Ulva walked out at No. 10.

With Manal Chauhan (47 off 32) he put on 57 for the ninth wicket. Then Manal fell, and only one wicket was left. Eight were needed off the final over. A dot, a wide, four singles. Three needed off the last ball.

Ulva hit it for FOUR. 49* off 26 balls.

India won by one wicket, their 9th U19 Asia Cup title.

Heroes aren't always at the top of the scorecard. 🇮🇳
