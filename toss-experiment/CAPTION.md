Does winning the toss actually win cricket matches? 🪙🏏

The numbers sit closer to 50–50 than most fans expect: toss winners won 54.3% of decided Tests, 49.6% of ODIs, 50.3% of T20Is and 51.6% in the IPL.

The real edge isn't the coin. It's the choice, and how much that choice is worth depends on conditions: day-night games, dew, and a Test pitch on day five.
