Roll a dice 🎲 Is it a six? Just yes or no. That simple question has its own distribution: BERNOULLI. And rolling until you finally get a six? That's the GEOMETRIC distribution.

📖 Bernoulli, in simple words:
1️⃣ ONE try
2️⃣ TWO outcomes: success or failure
3️⃣ Success = 1, failure = 0
4️⃣ It needs just one number: the chance of success

🎲 One roll: a six has a 1/6 chance (17%), anything else 5/6 (83%). Just two bars!

💻 Yes-or-no is everywhere in data science: did they click? Is it spam? Will they buy? Many ML models predict exactly this: the chance of a yes. Repeat the try and count the yeses → the binomial.

📖 Geometric, in simple words: it counts how many tries it takes to get the FIRST success. Like the exponential, it's about waiting, but it counts tries instead of time ⏱️

📊 Rolling until a six:
• 1st roll: about 17%
• 2nd roll: about 14%
• 3rd roll: about 12%
Each extra roll is a bit less likely. Surprise: the most likely roll is the very first one!

🎯 The average wait = 1 ÷ chance. A 1-in-6 chance → 6 rolls on average.
