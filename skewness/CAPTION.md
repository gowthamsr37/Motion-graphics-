The average salary in this city is ₹50,000… yet 7 out of 9 people earn LESS than that 🤯 How can most people be below average? 👀 The answer: SKEW.

📐 Skew = lopsided data. Most values pile up on one side, and a long tail stretches out the other way. The tail's direction names it.

The 3 shapes:
⚖️ Symmetric (heights): both sides match
➡️ Right-skewed (salaries): a few huge values, long tail to the right
⬅️ Left-skewed (easy test scores): most score high, a few trail off to the left

🧲 The key rule: the tail pulls the MEAN toward it
• Right skew: mode < median < mean
• Left skew: mean < median < mode
• Symmetric: they all meet in the middle

🔍 How to spot it: compare the mean and median (mean > median = right skew), or look for the longer box-plot whisker.

✅ What to do: in skewed data, report the median + IQR, or log-transform it.
