Is Rishabh Pant India's greatest keeper-batter? We ran the numbers against every Indian keeper and the world's best. 🧤🏏

💯 9 Test hundreds, the most by an Indian keeper. Dhoni made 6 in 90 Tests; Pant has 9 in 53.
📏 One hundred every 6 Tests. Dhoni: every 15. Saha: 13. Engineer: 23. Kirmani: 44.

🌍 Average v strike rate of the world's leading keepers: most sit in the 30s at 50–60 runs per 100 balls. Pant: 44.8 at 74.3. Only Adam Gilchrist (47.6 at 82) is higher and faster.

⚡ THE TEMPO GAP: Pant scores 25 runs per 100 balls faster than his own top-order teammates. Dhoni +6, Watling −11, Saha −8. Edgbaston 2022: with India 98/5, he made 146 off 111.

💥 108 Test sixes, the most by any wicketkeeper (Gilchrist 100, Dhoni 78).

🏠✈️ At home he averages 49.6 and scores at 99 per 100 balls against spin. Away, 42.9 (Dhoni 32.8). Since 2002, India's other keepers have 3 away hundreds between them; Pant has 7, all in SENA countries, at least one in each. Dhoni made none in SENA.

📉 THE HONEST PART: walking in at 4 down for under 150, Pant averages 34. De Kock (48) and Watling (43) do better.
📈 BUT in the 4th innings: 114 at the Oval, 97 to save Sydney, 89* to win at the Gabba, and 64 at Mumbai when the rest of the innings added 57. His 4th-innings average is 41 (Dhoni 33).
🛡️ In Tests his team lost, he still averages 39. Dhoni: 27. No keeper here fights harder in defeat.

By hundreds, tempo and away runs, nobody else is close. The gloves? That's another debate. 👇
