Three years. Seven No.3s. One answer. 🏏

Since Cheteshwar Pujara's last Test in June 2023, India's No.3 spot has been a revolving door. Then Devdutt Padikkal walked in.

His last three first-innings scores: 167 · 117 · 80.

📊 Average at No.3 since Pujara: Gill 37.4 · Sai Sudharsan 31.9 · Karun Nair 27.8 · PADIKKAL 72.2. Pujara's own career mark there: 44.4.

🛣️ The road: 65 on debut → a duck at Perth → 21 months out → 167 at Galle (first Test hundred) → 117 in Colombo → 80 in Wellington.

⏱️ A No.3 is measured in time. Balls faced per dismissal: Pujara 101 · the others since him 61 · Padikkal 128, and at a faster strike rate than Pujara (56.5 v 43.9).

🪞 Padikkal v Sai Sudharsan: mirror images in first-class cricket (averages 44.6 and 42.9, ten hundreds each). In Tests the mirror breaks: 71.1 v 31.9, two hundreds v none. Sai is only 25, and his time may come.

Seven innings is a small sample. But every number points the same way.
