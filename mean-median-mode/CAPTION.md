Your class average, a typical salary, the most popular shoe size… all "averages", but they don't always agree 👀

☕ 5 friends, cups of chai: 5, 2, 8, 2, 3
📊 MEAN: the fair share → 20 ÷ 5 = 4
📍 MEDIAN: the middle after sorting → 2, 2, 3, 5, 8 → 3
👑 MODE: the most common → 2 (and it works for categories: masala wins!)

⚠️ Twist: one friend drinks 38 cups. The mean jumps to 10, the median stays at 3. That's why salaries are often reported as the median.
