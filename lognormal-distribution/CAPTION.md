House prices in a city 🏠 Most are moderate, a few are very expensive, so the shape leans left with a long tail to the right. But change the scale and they form a perfect bell 🔔 That's the LOG-NORMAL DISTRIBUTION.

📖 The definition, in simple words:
1️⃣ Only POSITIVE values
2️⃣ SKEWED to the right
3️⃣ Take the log of every value → a NORMAL bell curve
That's the name: its logs are normal!

🔢 What's a log? It counts the zeros: log of 10 = 1, log of 100 = 2, log of 1,000 = 3. On a log scale, every step means ×10.

📊 100 house prices on a normal scale: bunched on the left, a few far right. Switch to a log scale: a symmetric bell.

📈 Why? Prices grow by percentages. A 10% rise adds ₹2 lakh to a ₹20 lakh flat but ₹20 lakh to a ₹2 crore villa. Growth that multiplies stretches the big values out: the long tail.

🌍 Incomes, house prices, time to finish a task, stock prices.

💡 Data science tip: when your data looks like this, take the log. Many models work better on a bell shape.
