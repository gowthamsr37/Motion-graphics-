973 runs. 27 short of a thousand. 8 short of a title. 🏏

In IPL 2016, Virat Kohli scored 973 runs in 16 innings at an average of 81.08 and a strike rate of 152.03. He made four centuries and seven fifties and won the Orange Cap.

The fourth hundred was 113 off 50 balls against Kings XI Punjab, in a rain-shortened 15-over match, with nine stitches in his left hand.

He was out for a duck in Qualifier 1, but RCB still made the final. There, Kohli made 54 off 35. SRH posted 208/7, RCB finished on 200/7, and the season ended eight runs short.

Some seasons win trophies. Some seasons become history.
