The ball smashes into the pad. It's hitting the stumps. And it's still NOT OUT. 🤯

LBW comes down to three questions:
1️⃣ PITCH: did it bounce outside leg stump? Then it's not out. Ever.
2️⃣ IMPACT: did it hit him in line with the stumps? (Outside off only counts if he didn't play a shot.)
3️⃣ WICKETS: would it have gone on to hit the stumps?

Three green lights, and the finger goes up. And remember: hit the bat first, or bowled a no-ball? Never LBW.
