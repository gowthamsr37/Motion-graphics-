A chai stall ☕ gets 4 customers an hour, on average. One just left. How long until the next one walks in? ⏱️ The EXPONENTIAL DISTRIBUTION answers that.

📖 The definition, in simple words:
1️⃣ It measures the WAITING TIME
2️⃣ until the NEXT EVENT
3️⃣ when events happen at random, at a steady average rate
4️⃣ Short waits are the most common; long waits get rarer and rarer

🚶 4 customers an hour means one every 15 minutes on average, but real gaps vary: 6 min, 22, 3, 31… Collect many gaps and the shape falls from left to right 📉

📊 With an average wait of 15 minutes:
• Next customer within 5 minutes: about 28%
• Longer than half an hour: only about 14%

😮 The surprise: the average gap is 15 minutes, yet about 63% of gaps are SHORTER. A few very long gaps pull the average up.

🧠 It has no memory: waited 10 minutes already? The next customer is no closer. From any moment, the wait ahead still averages 15 minutes.

🔗 Poisson counts the events. Exponential measures the gaps between them.
