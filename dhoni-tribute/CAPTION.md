The end of an era. 💛 MS Dhoni has retired from the IPL.

2008: the IPL's most expensive signing at $1.5 million. Chennai didn't just buy a player. They found their captain.

235 matches as captain. 136 wins. 10 finals. 5 titles: 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023.

5,439 runs at an average of 38.30 and a strike rate of 137.45. 24 fifties. 264 sixes.

205 wicketkeeping dismissals (158 catches, 47 stumpings), the most in IPL history.

At 42, in 2024, he still struck at 220.55.

His last IPL game was in May 2025. Injury kept him out of 2026. From 2027, he becomes Chennai's mentor.

Some legacies can't be measured in numbers. Thank you, Thala. 🙏
