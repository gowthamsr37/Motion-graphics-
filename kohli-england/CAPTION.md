2014: 134 runs in 10 innings. 2018: 149 in his very first innings. 🏏

Virat Kohli's 2014 Test tour of England was a nightmare: an average of 13.40, two ducks, no fifty, a highest score of 39, and four dismissals to James Anderson.

Four years later he came back more patient, leaving the ball better and waiting for the right delivery. At Edgbaston he made 149 in the first innings, 15 more than his whole 2014 series. He finished with 593 runs at 59.30, two hundreds and three fifties, and Anderson didn't dismiss him once.

India still lost the series 4–1, but Kohli had rewritten his own story.

Same country. Same challenge. A different batter.
