You found an outlier… so you just delete it? NOT SO FAST 👀 Delete the wrong one and you lose the most important point in your data.

🔍 Step 0: ask WHY it's there. Is it a mistake, or real but rare?

⚠️ If it's a MISTAKE
1️⃣ Fix it: age 250? The form says 25 ✅
2️⃣ Fill it in: can't recover it? Use the median (and keep the rest of the row)
3️⃣ Remove it: a test account doesn't belong. Remove it and note why 📝

💎 If it's REAL BUT RARE
4️⃣ Cap it (winsorizing): pull it back to the IQR fence (a 60-min jam becomes 42)
5️⃣ Transform it: a log scale squeezes a long tail (salaries, house prices)
6️⃣ Use robust tools: median + IQR instead of mean + SD
7️⃣ Keep it and study it: a ₹2 lakh payment at 3 AM could be fraud. The outlier IS the story 🚨

🏆 Golden rule: unsure? Run it with AND without the outlier, and report both.
