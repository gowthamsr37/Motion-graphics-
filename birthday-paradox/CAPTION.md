PARADOX #1: THE BIRTHDAY PARADOX

Most people guess about 6% (23 out of 365).
The real answer: 50.7%. A coin flip. With 70 people: 99.9%.

🧠 Why your gut is wrong
Your brain asks: "does someone share MY birthday?" That really is small (≈6%).
But the question is: do ANY two people share one? 23 people make 253 different pairs. 253 chances for a match.

🧮 The maths, step by step
Work out the opposite: everyone different.
Person 1: 365/365 · Person 2: 364/365 · Person 3: 363/365 … Person 23: 343/365
Multiply them all → 49.3% (all different)
100 − 49.3 = 50.7% (at least one match) ✅

📈 10 people: 12% · 23: 50% · 40: 89% · 57: 99%
💻 We simulated 1,000 rooms of 23 people: 507 had a shared birthday.

🔐 Real life: hackers use the "birthday attack" to find two files with the same digital fingerprint, which is why passwords and banks use very long codes. And in a class of 60? 99.4%.
