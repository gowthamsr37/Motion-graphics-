# Instagram caption

A chai stall ☕ gets 4 customers an hour, on average. What's the chance of 10 customers in one hour? 🤔 The POISSON DISTRIBUTION answers that.

📖 The definition, in simple words:
1️⃣ It counts how many times something happens
2️⃣ in a fixed stretch of time or space
3️⃣ when events happen at random, at a steady average rate
4️⃣ and it needs just one number: the average

⏱️ Hour by hour: 3 customers, then 5, then 4, then 2. Count many hours and a shape appears.

📊 For an average of 4:
• 3 and 4 customers are the most likely (about 20% each)
• 0 customers: about 2%
• 10 customers: only about 1 in 200

🔁 Change the average and the shape changes: at 1 an hour it's mostly 0 or 1; at 15 an hour it's wider and almost a bell 🔔

🌍 Used everywhere: calls to a help desk, website visits per minute, typos on a page, goals in a match ⚽
