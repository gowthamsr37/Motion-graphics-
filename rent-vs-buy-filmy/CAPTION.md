Arjun bought a flat. Meera rented and invested the difference. 20 years later, one of them is ₹1.7 crore richer… 🏠📈

But the real lesson isn't rent vs buy. In year 6 the market crashed 35% and everyone told Meera to sell. If she had panicked once, it would have cost her ₹1.5 crore.

And if Bangalore flat prices grew 8% a year instead of 5%, Arjun would have won.

Renting doesn't beat buying. Discipline does.
