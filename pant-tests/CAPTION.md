Rishabh Pant in Tests: the highs, the lows, the spin, and the nervous nineties. 🧤🏏

Today at Wellington he made 128 off 153 balls. It was his 9th Test hundred and his first in New Zealand, and it makes him the first Asian keeper with Test hundreds in all four SENA countries. He now has 108 Test sixes; only Ben Stokes has more.

HIGHS: off the mark with a six. A hundred at the Oval in his 3rd Test. 159* at Sydney. 89* to win at the Gabba, where Australia hadn't lost in 32 years.
LOWS: an average of 17.8 in 2020. Then the car crash on 30 Dec 2022, and 637 days between Tests. He made 109 in his comeback Test.
2025: twin hundreds at Leeds (only Andy Flower had done it as a keeper), then 54 at Manchester on a fractured foot.

SPIN: average 52.8 at a strike rate of 86, against 39.0 at 65 versus pace. 75 of his first 100 Test sixes came off spinners. At home against spin, he scores 99 runs per 100 balls.
LYON: dismissed him 6 times, more than any other bowler. Pant has taken 258 runs and 7 sixes off him.

OVERSEAS: away average 42.9 with 7 hundreds and a strike rate of 69. That's the best average, the most hundreds and the fastest scoring among the leading keepers since 2002.

THE NINETIES: 7 scores in the nineties, 9 hundreds. 44% of his 90-plus scores stopped short; across Test cricket it's 18%. Since 2002, only AB de Villiers and Alastair Cook have as many nineties, and they did it in more than twice as many Tests. His strike rate drops from 105 when he's on 70–89 to 73 in the nineties.
