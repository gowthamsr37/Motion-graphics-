## Data and method

- **Source:** [Cricsheet](https://cricsheet.org) Test ball-by-ball JSON (ODC-BY), downloaded on 16 Nov 2026. The scripts are in `analysis/` (`extract.py`, `analyse.py`, `keepers.py`, `career.py`) and the outputs are the JSON files there.
- **Added by hand:**
  - The June 2026 Afghanistan Test at Mullanpur, which Cricsheet withholds. Pant played and is reported to have made 81 (one secondary scorecard says 69). It counts in the career totals, not in any ball-level stat.
  - Today's 128 off 153 at Wellington.
- **Career totals:** 53 Tests, 91 innings, 3,853 runs, 86 dismissals, average 44.80, 9 hundreds, 7 nineties.
- **Sixes:** Cricsheet has exactly 100 before Wellington. The reported career total after Wellington is 108, second only to Ben Stokes (138). Reports disagree on how many sixes he hit today (6 or 8), so that number isn't shown.
- **Pace or spin:** each of the 80 bowlers he has faced was labelled by hand. Keshara Nuwantha (Sri Lanka, 2026) is an off-spinner; Usman Khawaja (2 balls) is left unclassified.
  - Against spin: 2,147 balls, 1,847 runs, 35 dismissals, 75 sixes.
  - Against pace: 2,753 balls, 1,795 runs, 46 dismissals, 25 sixes.
  - Dismissals here exclude run-outs.
- **Away record:**
  - Away means a Test outside the player's home country, worked out from the venue's city.
  - Keepers: the main designated keepers with 20+ away Tests in the data (Dec 2001 onward).
  - Gilchrist and Boucher are left out because their careers began before the data does.
  - Pant's line includes Wellington: 37 away Tests, 2,662 runs, 62 dismissals, 7 hundreds. Prior: 35 away Tests, 1,927 runs, 45 dismissals.
- **Nineties:**
  - The baseline is every Test innings in the data since Dec 2001: 17.6% of scores of 90+ ended between 90 and 99.
  - Pant: 7 of 16 (43.8%).
  - Strike rates are measured on balls faced while his score was 70–89 and 90–99, from ball-by-ball data.
- **Background facts:**
  - The 30 Dec 2022 car crash. The gap from his last Test before it (22 Dec 2022) to his first after it (19 Sep 2024) is 637 days.
  - Gabba 2021: Australia's first defeat there since 1988.
  - Andy Flower was the first keeper to score a hundred in each innings of a Test.
  - Dhoni's 6 Test hundreds.
