In many shops, just 20% of customers bring in 80% of the sales 🛍️ That's not luck, it's a pattern: the PARETO DISTRIBUTION.

📖 The definition, in simple words:
1️⃣ Most values are SMALL
2️⃣ A few values are HUGE
3️⃣ So the shape has a LONG TAIL to the right
4️⃣ It's the idea behind the famous 80/20 rule

📊 Line up a shop's customers from biggest to smallest spender and split them into 10 groups:
• The top 10% bring in 73% of all sales
• The top 20% bring in 80%
• The whole bottom half? Only about 9%

📏 Heights cluster around an average: nobody is 10× taller than you. But one customer can spend 100× more than another. That's the long tail: rare, but enormous values.

⚠️ The average trap: the average customer spends about ₹1,400, but the typical customer spends only about ₹360. 9 out of 10 spend LESS than the average! For long-tailed data, the median tells you more.

🌍 Wealth, city sizes, video views, software bugs: a few hold most.

✋ 80/20 is a rule of thumb, not a law: it can be 90/10 or 70/30.
