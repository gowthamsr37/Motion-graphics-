Forget the hundreds. Watch what happens to a Test when Rishabh Pant walks in. 🏏⚡

⏱️ THE TEMPO: since his debut, top-order Test batters score at 53 runs per 100 balls. Pant scores at 74. With him at the crease, India score at 3.89 an over; the rest of the time, 3.16.

⌛ SPEED IS TIME: batting at an ordinary pace, his runs would have needed 323 more overs. That's 3.6 days of Test cricket handed back to India's bowlers.

🔥 WHEN THE MATCH IS SLIPPING:
• Sydney 2021, chasing 407: 97 off 118 while his partners made 45 off 146. Test saved.
• The Gabba 2021, chasing 328: 89*. Chase won, series won.
• Ahmedabad 2021: in at 80/4, out with 101. India win by an innings.

💥 THE SIX: 108 in Tests, second only to Ben Stokes, and almost 1 in 5 of India's Test sixes since 2018. 75 of his first 100 came off spin. Nathan Lyon got him six times; Pant took 258 runs and 7 sixes off him.

❤️‍🩹 THE COMEBACK: a car crash on 30 Dec 2022. 637 days later, a hundred at Chennai in his first Test back, and India won. Then a hundred in each innings at Leeds, and batting on a fractured foot at Manchester.

🌍 Away from home, no leading keeper of the last two decades averages more, has more hundreds, or scores faster.

The price of the same instinct: out in the nineties 7 times, against 9 hundreds.

Don't judge him by his average. Judge him by the match. 👇
