Your friend's coin 🪙 lands heads 9 times out of 10. Is it rigged, or was it just luck? HYPOTHESIS TESTING helps you decide.

📖 The definition, in simple words: use data to decide whether an effect is REAL or just CHANCE.
1️⃣ First, ASSUME nothing special is going on
2️⃣ Then check: is the data too SURPRISING for that to be true?
3️⃣ If it is, reject that assumption

⚖️ Two hypotheses:
• The NULL (H0): nothing's going on. The coin is fair.
• The ALTERNATIVE (H1): something is going on. The coin favours heads.

👩‍⚖️ It works like a courtroom: the null is "innocent until proven guilty". Only strong evidence lets us reject it. Weak evidence means "not guilty", which is NOT the same as "innocent".

🎲 How surprising is 9 heads? If the coin were fair, 9 or more heads in 10 flips happens only about 1% of the time.

📏 The decision: before testing, choose a line, usually 5%.
• 1% is below the line → reject the null. The coin is probably rigged.
• 6 heads happens about 38% of the time → fail to reject. But that doesn't PROVE the coin is fair.
