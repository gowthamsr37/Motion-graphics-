What if Virat Kohli played for Pakistan, and Babar Azam for India? 🔄🏏

We replayed every ODI they've played since May 2015 (292 matches), 10,000 times each.

📊 Since Babar's debut: Kohli averages 65.5 (SR 98), Babar 52.4 (SR 87). That's 13 more runs every innings.

🎯 The gap is in the chase. Batting first: Kohli 63.1, Babar 55.9. Chasing: Kohli 67.9, Babar 49.1.

🧪 A fair test first: each man replaced by a random innings of his own (the luck-free baseline). India win 101 of 154, Pakistan 72 of 138.

🔄 THE SWAP:
🇮🇳 India with Babar: 95 wins (about 6 fewer, more than half of them in chases)
🇵🇰 Pakistan with Kohli: 76.5 wins (4.5 more, about one extra win every 30 games)

Why not more? Only 44 of the 292 games were decided by less than 13 runs.

🚪 Sliding doors:
• Asia Cup 2023, Colombo: Pakistan lost to Sri Lanka off the last ball and missed the final. With Kohli, they win 66% of replays.
• World Cup 2019 semi-final, Manchester: Kohli made 1. With Babar, India win 19% of replays.

⚔️ India v Pakistan: in the 6 ODIs both played, India won 5. Swapped, India still win 5. The rivalry was never just about Kohli.

Verdict: Kohli is worth about 5 more ODI wins than Babar across a decade. Swap the shirts and the gap narrows. It doesn't close.

How it works: for every match, one man's innings is replaced by one of the other's, drawn at random from his own record in the same situation (batting first or chasing, v top teams or the rest). Teammates bat any balls he leaves at their own rate; a winning chase keeps the runs its wickets and balls in hand were worth. Results are compared with the luck-free baseline.
